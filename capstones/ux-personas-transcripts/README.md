# UX Capstone — User personas + transcripts (AI-generated)

**Assignment (video brief):** Generate detailed user personas for the capstone product and
simulate real user interview transcripts with AI (DeepSeek/Claude are fine), as detailed as you can.

## Personas (CareMate)

### P1 — Sana, 38, adult daughter + caregiver
Books and reschedules 2 specialists for her mother. Time-poor, anxious about losing slots.
Tools: clinic portals, phone, paper reminders. Wants: one timeline, text confirmations.
Frustration: no confirmation before changes are saved.

### P2 — Tunde, 52, husband caring for his wife
Manages weekly PT + monthly follow-ups. Not technical; prefers the phone. Trusts people, not apps.
Wants: a human pause — “are you sure?” before booking. Frustration: portals with no call option.

## Simulated interview transcripts (AI-generated, then reviewed by me)

### Sana (excerpt)
> R: “Walk me through the last time you changed an appointment.”
> S: “I called the clinic, they put me on hold, then I had to call the pharmacy to move a pickup. I never *knew* if it stuck until the reminder text.”
> **→ Finding: wants confirmation before save (trust builder).**

### Tunde (excerpt)
> R: “What would make you trust an app for this?”
> T: “If it told me what it was going to do before doing it — and could call a human if it messed up.”
> **→ Finding: confirmation governor, designed failure with retry.**

Both transcripts were generated via a single AI prompt seeded with the personas (“generate a
8-message interview with Sana about rescheduling; include a moment where she fears losing a slot”),
then I edited for specific, believable detail and tagged the design implication each line supports.

## Deliverable check
- Personas as detailed as possible → P1, P2 with jobs/tools/frustrations.
- Simulated transcripts of real users via AI → Sana + Tunde excerpts linking to design findings.
