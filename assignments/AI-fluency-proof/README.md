# AI Fluency — Week 1 · What are you proving (narrow claim)

**Deliverable:** one honest line on why this needs to exist / what you can't prove today with a CV
or LinkedIn alone that owning this fixes. Written by using AI as a thinking partner (not an author).

## The interview (AI as thinking partner — sharp turns)
1. "What's the one thing you want a stranger to remember in 5 seconds?" → *"That I ship real things."*
2. "But CVs are full of shipped things — what's different?" → *"Anyone can list an internship. I can show passing capstone work with tests and a live deployment."*
3. "What can't a CV or LinkedIn prove?" → *"That the work is mine, runs, and was reviewed-green — end to end."*

After three rounds of push-back, the claim got narrow and *mine*:

## The proof statement (one line)
> **"I build reliable backend systems and AI integrations that remove manual work — proven by the
> finished, review-passing FlyRank capstones I shipped, not by a certificate."**

## Why this needs to exist (one honest line)
> A CV proves employment history; owning this proves **quality of work** — the standard a hiring
> engineer would actually judge, and the gap no LinkedIn card closes.

## Deliverable check
- AI used as thinking partner, claim is narrow and true → the narrowing chain above.
- One honest line on why it needs to exist → final line.
