# UX-01 — Research, IA, Flows & Wireframes (project pitch)

**Project theme (CareMate — telehealth):** busy caregivers who juggle appointments for
relatives. Platform: mobile web. Success criteria: an appointment that once took 3 phone calls
is booked in under 90 seconds.

## 5-sentence pitch
CareMate is a mobile-first booking assistant for people who manage healthcare for someone else.
Our user is the unpaid caregiver (spouse, adult child) who books, reschedules, and juggles
appointments across providers. The problem: scheduling is fragmented across portals, phone lines
and paper reminders. We ship a companion app where the same _AI booking assistant_ can
reschedule 2+ appointments, confirm changes before they’re saved, and keep a single upcoming-visit
timeline. Success = a caregiver books or changes a visit in under 90 seconds without a phone call;
we’ll measure median task time and ask-again rate.
