- **Proof statement (given to the reviewer first):**

  > finished, review-passing FlyRank capstones I shipped, not by a certificate."
- **Reviewer asked to judge:** does the page move one person from landing to *believing* to *the
  one action* (contact with proof)?

## Real feedback received
> "The hero keeps the claim in one line, and the case cards are concrete — the only confusion is:
> there are two entry points (CV and Contact), and the accent draws me to links rather than a
> single primary action. Consider one emphasized CTA instead of two equal ones."

## What I changed because of it
1. **One primary action:** the accent now points to the booking/mail CTA as the single emphasized
   action; other links are neutral (muted text, underline on hover).
2. **Reduced "two equal doors":** CV stays a thin secondary link under the action, not a competing button.

Both changes are reflected in the deployed site and its source (`PF-04-personal-website/site/index.html`).
